About Me

Curious about the world above, below, and beyond.

Trondheim, Norway

Hi, I'm Sigurd Rolfsnes. I am a third-year industrial mathematics student pursuing an integrated master's degree in applied physics and mathematics.

Alongside my studies, I am an instructor at NTNUI Gymnastics. I love the mix of precision, movement, and creativity that both gymnastics and photography bring into my life.

When I am not studying or coaching, you will usually find me looking for adventures, exploring striking landscapes, or pointing my camera toward space. Photography is how I share the wonder I find in all of it.
