About Me

Living with an intrisic curiousity about the world above and around us

Trondheim, Norway

Hi, I'm Sigurd Rolfsnes. I am a 22 year old third-year industrial mathematics student pursuing an integrated master's degree in applied physics and mathematics.

Alongside my studies, I am an instructor and member at NTNUI Gymnastics. Coaching and training there is one of the best parts of my student life at NTNU.

When I am not studying or doing gymnastics, you will usually find me looking for adventures, exploring crazy landscapes, or pointing my camera toward space. Photography is how I share the wonder and awe I find in all of it.
